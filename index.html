01 / THE WORK BEHIND THE TITLE

The whole workflow
is the product.

I'm the AI Chief of Staff at Replit, based in San Francisco. I lead AI Studio, where I work with teams to turn operational problems into products built around the way they actually work.

I build internal tools because the gaps between systems become work for people: finding an answer, coordinating a trip, reviewing a contract, assembling account context. I connect that context and design the next step into the product, from the first conversation through the rollout, teaching, and continued improvement.

12 PRODUCTS
TravelPolicy & spendWeb + iOS

Every part of the journey, connected.

A corporate travel platform connecting trips, flights, hotels, policy, approvals, and spend across web and native iOS.

Why it matters. Travelers need a clear next step. Operations and finance need the same picture, especially when plans change.

PeopleResourcesEmployee experience

A company that is easier to navigate.

An employee home for company resources, people, teams, org structure, office information, and everyday answers.

Why it matters. Finding the right person or document should not depend on knowing which channel to ask in.

SearchCitationsAccess-aware AI

Find the answer. Keep the evidence.

A company search and intelligence workspace for finding decisions, prior work, source material, and the people behind them.

Why it matters. Useful answers need traceable sources and the right access, not another layer of information to sort through.

SupportProduct signalsIncident response

Turn customer signals into coordinated action.

A support workspace connecting queue health, customer sentiment, quality, reporting, knowledge gaps, and incident response.

Why it matters. Support teams need to see what needs attention and connect individual issues to patterns across the product.

LegalContract reviewHuman judgment

Give legal judgment a better workspace.

AI-assisted contract review bringing clause triage, playbooks, redlines, precedent, and negotiation context into one workspace.

Why it matters. Legal teams need the relevant evidence beside the clause they are reviewing, with control over every decision.

RevenuePipelineAccount context

Less assembly. More informed next steps.

A shared workspace for revenue priorities, pipeline, forecasts, account research, and AI-assisted daily work.

Why it matters. Teams should be able to act on account context without rebuilding it from disconnected systems every morning.
